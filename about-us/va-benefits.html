Dental VA Benefits

We are proud to accept Dental VA Benefits and welcome eligible veterans to the practice of Don B. Ruhnke, DDS. If you live or work in the Kingsville area, receiving your care locally provides a convenient, comfortable option for your dental needs.

If you have any questions about how to utilize your benefits, feel free to give us a call at (361) 592-8521, and our team will be happy to help guide you.


Thank you for your service to our country!
