Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Everyone deserves a smile they feel confident sharing! We are pleased to offer Invisalign® clear aligners to help you achieve a straighter, healthier smile discreetly. Invisalign uses custom-crafted, clear aligners generated by advanced digital imaging to gradually and comfortably shift your teeth into proper alignment over time.

For patients requiring more complex or extensive orthodontic care, we work closely with trusted local orthodontists to ensure you receive the specialized treatment you need.

Ready to explore your options for a straighter smile? Contact the office of Don B. Ruhnke, DDS today at (361) 592-8521 to schedule your orthodontic consultation in Kingsville, TX!
