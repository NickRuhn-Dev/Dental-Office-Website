Dental Implants

A dental implant is an advanced, permanent replacement for a missing tooth root. Biocompatibly placed into the jawbone beneath the gumline, an implant provides a stable foundation for single crowns, fixed bridges, partial dentures, or complete dentures. Beyond restoring full chewing function and smile aesthetics, placing a dental implant—especially shortly after a tooth extraction—helps preserve your natural jawbone structure and prevents the bone loss that naturally occurs when teeth are missing.

Dr. Don B. Ruhnke, DDS provides both the surgical placement and custom restoration of dental implants conveniently in our Kingsville, TX office. For complex cases involving extensive bone grafting, we coordinate closely with top local specialists to ensure you receive the highest standard of care and optimal long-term results.

Implant Crowns & Bridges

Implant crowns and bridges are securely screwed or cemented onto dental implants, often utilizing a custom abutment as a connecting post. Unlike traditional bridges that require reshaping adjacent enamel, implant-supported restorations replace missing teeth without altering or weakening neighboring healthy teeth. An implant bridge can replace multiple missing teeth in a row, using implants as solid anchor points.

Implant-Supported Partials & Dentures

  • Implant-Supported Partials: Adding implants beneath a partial denture provides extra stability and can often eliminate the need for visible metal wire clasps around neighboring teeth.
  • Removable Implant Dentures: Implants lock traditional dentures securely into place to prevent slipping and discomfort, while still allowing you to easily remove the appliance for daily cleaning.
  • Fixed Screw-Retained Dentures: For maximum chewing power and convenience, a full arch of teeth can be permanently anchored to implants, creating a non-removable solution that functions and feels like natural teeth.
