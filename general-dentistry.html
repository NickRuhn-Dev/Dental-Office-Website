Our general dentistry services focus on preventing, evaluating, diagnosing, and treating conditions of the oral cavity while preserving the function and natural appearance of your smile.

General Dentistry Services We Offer

Oral Exams

In addition to everyday home care, seeing your dentist every six months for a comprehensive oral exam and health evaluation is essential. Routine checkups allow us to detect issues early and provide timely, conservative care.

Teeth Cleaning

Optimal oral health relies on regular professional cleanings. Even with diligent brushing and flossing at home, professional cleanings target hard-to-reach areas to keep your mouth fresh while preventing tooth decay and gum disease.

Bridges

Dental bridges effectively restore gaps left by missing teeth. Supported by adjacent natural teeth or implants, custom bridges anchor sturdy replacement teeth into place to restore full chewing function and aesthetic balance.

Dental X-Rays

Digital X-rays are an essential tool for diagnosing dental conditions that may not be visible during a routine exam. They help us identify cavities, infections, and other issues in their early stages, allowing for more conservative and effective treatment.

Fillings

Cavity fillings repair tooth structure compromised by decay or wear. Using high-quality materials, a custom filling preserves your tooth's structural integrity, stops decay progression, and restores comfortable chewing surfaces.

Crowns

Dental crowns fit securely over damaged or heavily worn teeth to restore strength, protection, and appearance. Custom crowns blend naturally with your smile and provide long-lasting durability for years to come.

Why You Should Visit Your Dentist

Oral health directly impacts your overall well-being. Medical research links persistent oral infections to serious systemic health issues, including cardiovascular disease. Maintaining the health of your teeth, gums, and supporting tissues protects far more than just your smile. Building great oral health habits early creates a foundation for a lifetime of wellness. Children benefit from early dental visits to track growing smiles, prevent early decay, and become comfortable in a warm clinical environment. Every stage of life brings unique dental needs, and Dr. Ruhnke is dedicated to guiding you through each one.
