Pediatric Dental Services

At the practice of Don B. Ruhnke, DDS, we believe the foundation for a lifetime of healthy teeth begins early in life. For our youngest patients, we start with a welcoming "happy visit" to introduce them to the office environment. Whether you have young children or teenagers, our goal is to establish positive oral health habits and routines that support both parents and kids.

We want children to feel comfortable and excited about their visits. Our team is passionate about dental education, teaching good oral habits early to set the stage for long-term health. We are realists, too—kids love snacks and treats, so our approach focus on positive encouragement rather than nagging!

A Gentle, Kid-Friendly Approach

  • Comfort-Focused Team: Our doctor and staff provide a warm, gentle touch designed to build trust and eliminate fear.
  • No-Shot Treatments: Whenever possible, conservative procedures are offered without the need for shots.
  • Positive Reinforcement: We offer fun rewards and prizes to keep kids motivated about their dental care.

Most children have a fantastic experience when introduced to dental care in a supportive environment. To ensure safety and a positive outlook, we do not restrain or sedate children. For patients who require extensive care or specialized management beyond routine care, we work closely with trusted pediatric specialists. Our office welcomes new young patients ages 6 and up.
