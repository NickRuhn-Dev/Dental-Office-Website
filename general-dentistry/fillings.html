Tooth Restorations & Fillings

Dental fillings are used to restore a tooth when a portion of it is missing or affected by decay. Dr. Ruhnke removes the decayed or damaged area and replaces it with a durable filling material right here in Kingsville, TX. Beyond decay, fillings can repair teeth that have chipped or broken—such as from biting on hard food—or correct areas affected by wear and erosion. Fillings are most effective when decay is small to medium-sized, leaving ample healthy tooth structure to support the restoration. If decay is extensive or the tooth is cracked, a crown (or "cap") may be recommended, while deeper decay reaching the nerve may require pulp capping or root canal therapy.

There are several filling materials available, with tooth-colored composite resin being the most popular for matching your natural smile. Dental fillings are typically completed in a single convenient appointment. With proper oral hygiene, daily flossing, balanced eating habits, and regular preventative care, composite fillings can last for years before needing routine replacement.

Cosmetic Dental Bonding

Dental bonding utilizes shade-matched adhesive composite resin and a special curing light to seamlessly blend restorations with your natural teeth. Bonding is often used for cosmetic enhancements, such as closing gaps, repairing small chips, or reshaping teeth to improve the symmetry and appearance of your smile.
