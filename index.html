Comprehensive, Compassionate Dental Care for Your Entire Family

We are proud to welcome new and returning patients to our Kingsville office! If you have been searching for a dedicated dental team that places your overall well-being, comfort, and smile at the very center of your care, you have found your dental home right here with Dr. Don Ruhnke. Our goal is simple: to provide high-quality, modern dentistry in a warm, friendly environment where you always feel like family. We offer a comprehensive range of services—from routine preventative cleanings to advanced restorations—designed to keep your teeth healthy and your smile radiant.

Why choose our office?

  • Respect for Your Time: We know your schedule is busy, which is why we prioritize prompt appointments and efficient care to complete your treatments in as few visits as possible.
  • Your Comfort First: We understand that visiting the dentist can feel intimidating for some. We focus on gentle techniques, a relaxed atmosphere, and an empathetic approach to make every visit stress-free.
  • Dedicated Doctor Time: Dr. Ruhnke takes the time to sit down with you, listen to your concerns, and answer every question so you can make confident decisions about your oral health.
  • State-of-the-Art Technology: We utilize the latest dental technology for accurate diagnosis and treatment.

Meet Dr. Don Ruhnke

"We love to see you smile. Really, we do. Every great smile is our sign that we've delivered the highest level of care possible." — Dr. Don Ruhnke

A proud Kingsville native, Dr. Don Ruhnke has been serving the dental needs of his hometown and surrounding communities since 1987. Born right here at the old Kleberg County Hospital, Dr. Ruhnke's roots in Kingsville run deep, and his passion for helping his neighbors smile has only grown over his decades of practice. Dr. Ruhnke combines high-level technical expertise with a gentle, patient-first approach. He takes great pride in offering advanced dental care in a relaxed, friendly atmosphere where every patient feels at home. Dedicated to lifelong learning, he regularly completes continuing education courses to bring the latest, most comfortable, and technologically advanced treatments to Kingsville.
