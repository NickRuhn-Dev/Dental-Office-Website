What to Expect During Your New Patient Visit

Your first visit with us is designed to be comfortable, efficient, and tailored to your needs. From convenient check-ins to relaxed examinations, we make taking care of your smile seamless from start to finish.

Streamlined Check-In & Paperwork

We respect your time and aim to keep paperwork to a minimum. You can conveniently complete most of your registration online before your visit. If you prefer to fill out forms in person, we recommend arriving 15-20 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment to ensure plenty of time for your dental care.

Comfortable Digital X-Rays

Your visit begins with state-of-the-art digital X-rays taken quickly and comfortably from outside your mouth—eliminating bulky film while using the lowest possible radiation dosage.

Relaxing Examination & Diagnostics

Next, you will be escorted to a treatment room equipped with a plush leather massage chair to help you unwind. We will complete digital charting and perform a thorough head and neck examination to evaluate your overall oral health.

Dedicated Doctor Time & Personalized Plans

Dr. Ruhnke spends quality, dedicated time with you to listen, answer questions, and discuss your goals. You will receive a clear, comprehensive treatment plan along with alternative options, plain-language explanations, and upfront fee details—with zero pressure to proceed until you feel fully confident.

Amenities for Your Comfort

To ensure a great experience, we offer complimentary beverages, Wi-Fi, television, noise-reducing headphones, and massage chairs in every treatment room. Our goal is for you to leave your initial visit with a great impression and total peace of mind. Have questions before your visit? Call our team at (361) 592-8521—we look forward to welcoming you!
